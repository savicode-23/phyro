This first Loopline preview is for artists trying vector drawing and 2D animation. Extract the archive for your desktop and open `lantern-walk.vanim` to try an animated character.

### What to try

- Draw Bézier paths or use the freehand pencil, edit nodes, and combine shapes with Boolean shortcuts. Rename parts in Layers and batch export selected objects or whole groups as separate SVGs or PNGs.
- Combine frame-by-frame effects with animated symbols and nested clips. Open `draw-sparks-over-motion.vanim` to draw over a moving paper plane.
- Use `loopline-cli describe` to discover commands for inspecting, editing, and exporting projects from a script or AI agent.

Loopline keeps local recovery drafts for unsaved work. After an interrupted session, choose **Recover a copy**, then **Save As** to keep it. To report a crash, open **Help → Crash reports** and choose **Copy report**.
