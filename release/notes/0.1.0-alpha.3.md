Phyro 0.1.0-alpha.3 adds blend modes, grayscale preview, and more control over groups and curves.

## Drawing and editing

- Choose from 16 blend modes for layers and objects. Set separate modes for a path’s fill and stroke, including gradients. Blend modes carry through PNG and SVG export.
- Press **Shift+G** or use **Grayscale** beside **Fit** to check values on the canvas. Saved artwork and exports use the original colors.
- Right-click a group and choose **Enter group** to edit its contents. A breadcrumb and canvas outline show which group is active. **Ctrl-click** with Select to pick a single member directly.
- Press **Ctrl+L** to simplify a path. Phyro refits and smooths Bézier spans while protecting sharp corners, open endpoints, and nearby holes. Pencil and Boolean operations also retain cleaner curves with fewer unnecessary nodes.

## Rendering and reliability

- Sharper transform previews for artwork and placed clips, with less redundant canvas work during editing.
- Fixes for path editing, undo, drawing exposures, and selection and export workflows.

Earlier `.vanim` files still open. Projects saved with a blend mode require alpha.3 or later.
